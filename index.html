SURVIVAL GUIDE

  • Pan Camera: Move your mouse or drag horizontally on mobile screens to look around the office.
  • Doors & Lights: Use the side controls or physical wall buttons to switch door lights or seal doors.
  • Monitor Tablet: Tap/click the bottom bar to open security feeds (CAM 1A, 1B, 1C, 2A, 4A, 5) and track movement.
  • Power Management: Doors, lights, and cameras drain battery. Reach 0% and you will be left in total darkness.
  • Objective: Survive from 12:00 AM to 6:00 AM.
